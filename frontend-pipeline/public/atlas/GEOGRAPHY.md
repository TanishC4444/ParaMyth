# Globe geography

Coastlines and countries: Natural Earth 1:50m, distributed by world-atlas 2.0.2.

`hydrography.json` contains Natural Earth 1:50m rivers / lake centerlines (462
features) and lakes (412 features). Non-geographic feature properties were
removed; the original coordinates are preserved. Downloaded October 24, 2026:

- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_50m_rivers_lake_centerlines.geojson
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_50m_lakes.geojson

Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The 4096 × 2048 globe texture is generated locally from these vector layers.
Country colors are decorative; they do not encode political or story categories.
Story markers retain the separate literary-tradition palette.
